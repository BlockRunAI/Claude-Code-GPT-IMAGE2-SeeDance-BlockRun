## Original prompt

A talk show dialogue in the "Cat and Dog Roast Room" with rich emotion, fitting talk show performance:
Meow Sauce (cat host, licking fur and rolling eyes): "Family, who understands? This one next to me, all day long besides wagging tail and destroying the sofa, just uses that 'I'm super good, pet me please' eye to trick humans for snacks. When destroying the house, he's fiercer than anyone, yet has the nerve to call himself Wang Zai. I think 'Wang Chai' fits better haha."
Wang Zai (dog host, tilting head and wagging tail): "You dare say that about me? You sleep 18 hours a day, wake up and rub against human legs for canned food. Shed so much fur that human's black clothes are covered in your hair. After they sweep, you turn around and roll on the sofa again. Yet you dare act like a cold noble?"
