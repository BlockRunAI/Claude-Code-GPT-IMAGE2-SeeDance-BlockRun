## Original prompt

An old monkey sits at a milk tea shop counter, wiping his tools. The monkey orders from the server in Sichuan dialect: "Little sister, do you have Farewell My Concubine?"
Cut to close-up.
The server puts down what she's doing and looks at the old monkey strangely before responding: "No, how about American?"
Cut to the monkey.
He scratches his head and mutters: "No problem... wait, I do have a problem! My grandson sent me to buy milk tea, just ordered this Farewell My Concubine thing."
